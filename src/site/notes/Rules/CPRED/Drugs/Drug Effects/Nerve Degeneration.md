#drug-effect
Add to your Nerve Degeneration tracker in Addictions on your Character Sheet.

**[[Rules/Homebrew\|Homebrew]]**
- [[Rules/CPRED/Cyberpunk RED\|Cyberpunk RED]]:
	**Once you reach Nerve Degeneration 5, reset it and lower INT, REF or DEX by -1.**

- [[Rules/NCOverdrive/Neon City Overdrive\|Neon City Overdrive]]:
	**Once you reach Nerve Degeneration 5, reset it and add a new [[Rules/NCOverdrive/Damage/Traumas\|Trauma]].**
