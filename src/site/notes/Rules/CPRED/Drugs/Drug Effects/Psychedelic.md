**Drug Effect:** Roll Strength or less on d6 or suffer from hallucinations. GM decides if its a good or a bad trip.

On a good trip, lower [[Rules/CPRED/Cyberware/Cyberware#Cyberpsychosis\|Cyberpsychos Tracker]] by d6.
On a bad trip, lower your COOL by -2.
