A type of [[Rules/CPRED/Drugs/Drugs\|street drug]] that comes in form of black pills used for the clinical treatment of [[Lore/Cyberpsychosis\|Cyberpsychosis]][^1]. Prolonged use is associated with Nerve Degeneration, with many pacients ending up bed-ridden or empty-headed.

### Main Effect
- Lowers Cyberpsychosis Tracker by up to -5

### Side Effect
- Check DR 17 Resist Torture/Drugs; on a failure, reduce the main effect by 1 (from -5 to -4, from -4 to -3 and so on, up to 0)

### After effects
- If the [[Rules/CPRED/Drugs/Omega Blockers#Side Effect\|Resist T/D]] is failed, suffer from **Nerve Degeneration.** Once you reach Nerve Degeneration 5, reset it and lower INT, REF or DEX by -1.

[^1]: See [[Rules/CPRED/Cyberware\|Cyberware]] for additional details.
