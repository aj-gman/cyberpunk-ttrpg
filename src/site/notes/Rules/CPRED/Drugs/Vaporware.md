> [[Rules/CPRED/Quality\|Common Price:]] Premium (100-500eb)

A type of [[Rules/CPRED/Drugs/Drugs\|street drug]] that is smoked in its vapor form, traditionally by [hookah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hookah).
Commonly believed to be a mix between a strain of synthtobacco and opium seeds.

Vaporware is popular with [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Netrunner\|'runners]] as **it increases Concentration and decreases stress**, producing a feeling of separation between the person's psyche and emotions. This has been known to throw less-than-stable users over the edge of psychosis.

### Main Effect
- **INT: +4**
- **COOL: +4**
- Drug Strength: 8

### Side Effect
- **Alienation & Egotism:** +d6 on [[Rules/CPRED/Cyberware/Cyberware#Cyberpsychosis\|Cyberpsychosis Tracker]]
- **Test Addiction:** [[Rules/CPRED/Difficulty Rating\|Professional/Heroic]] depending on ingestion ([hookah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hookah)/[slap patch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transdermal_patch))  

### After Effects
- **After effects:** Hunger (INT-8 or EAT. If eat, suffer from [[Rules/CPRED/Drugs/Drug Effects/Drowsiness\|Drowsiness]], -8 Perception, -4 INT, -2 REF)
- **[[Rules/CPRED/Drugs/Drug Effects/Flashbacks\|Flashbacks]]** (test COOL or stunned for 1 round, twice per dose taken)
