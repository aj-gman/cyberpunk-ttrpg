Set in [[2045\|2045]], during the "Time of the Red".

## Roles
There are 10 core roles in the Cyberpunk TTRPG across all of its version. I like to split them by lines of combat-oriented, tech-oriented and social-oriented, although these lines are not as rigid as it may seem. An interesting [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Medtechie\|Medtechie]] might also be a drug dealer, while a [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Nomad\|Nomad]] might be so proficient in mechanics that it would be faulty to not consider him technologically adept.
