...is the equipment and weapons your character takes on jobs. Gear can be either basic or specialised. 

**Basic gear** is described with just its name, like heavy pistol or medipack.
**Specialised gear** has one or more tags listed after it, like sniper rifle (scope, silenced). A character can have a maximum **of four items of specialised gear.** Specialised gear may provide Action Dice.

[[Rules/CPRED/Cyberware/Cyberware\|Cyberware]] is a type of **Specialised Gear** that has **a separate maximum** and is linked with a [[Rules/CPRED/Cyberware/Cyberpsychosis\|Cyberpsychosis]] [[Rules/Homebrew\|Homebrew]] mechanic.
