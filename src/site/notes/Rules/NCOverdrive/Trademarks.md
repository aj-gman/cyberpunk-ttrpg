A **Trademark** defines a character's background, profession, signature equipment, or unique abilities.

Each contains a **Name**, a list of **[[Rules/NCOverdrive/Triggers\|Triggers]]**, and example **[[Rules/NCOverdrive/Flaws\|Flaws]]**.

Players can adjust **[[Rules/NCOverdrive/Triggers\|Triggers]]** to fit their character concept. Major mechanical changes require discussion with the **GM** and other players.
