> **Note from G-man:** [[z-SITE/Homepage\|G-man's Cyberpunk]] wouldn't be possible 5 years ago, as I've never been technically-proficient. I've always focused more on the craft of storytelling, narration and production. In all my creative endeavors (writing, filmmaking, gamemastering, game design) I wasn't exactly the best at the technical expects, although I've always tried my best. With [[z-SITE/Homepage\|G-man's Cyberpunk]] and its predecessors (the [Miraheze Wiki](https://gmantabletops.miraheze.org/wiki/Main_Page) & the [Fandom Wiki](https://gmanwod.fandom.com/wiki/G-MAN%27s_WORLD_OF_DARKNESS_Wiki)) I've had the opportunity to use AI as a tool, to troubleshoot bugs, to break the mold of the platform in order to engineer ideas that weren't common. I don't like the way AI has been created, I don't like how its marketed or its negative effects on the world, but if I can use a tiny fraction of that evil to bring light in a small corner of the globe through the internet, I think that it just might be worth it. That is, until we pull [a Johnny Silverhand](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Night_City_Holocaust) on them all or, even better, pull [a Rache Bartmoss](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/DataKrash) on 'em and run our shit locally.

- AI tools like Google Gemini and Claude AI have been used to transform ideas into lines of code (HTML, JavaScript, CSS etc.);
	- As such, we do not claim any authorship of the code of this website;
- AI tools have not aided in the creation/modification of creative content like images or text;
	- While we sometimes use AI-modified content in our Sessions, none of it is made public, as it alters the original artist's content without their consent
	- All text and images available on [[z-SITE/Homepage\|G-man's Cyberpunk]] is humanmade.
