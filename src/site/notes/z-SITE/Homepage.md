Welcome to [G-man](https://gmantabletops.miraheze.org/wiki/User:AidenJacks)'s Cyberpunk [Digital Garden](https://docs.forestry.md/)! 
This **Wiki** is dedicated to preserving the archive of stories, rules and lore that gather during the many [TTRPG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tabletop_role-playing_game) sessions ran by [[G-man\|G-man]] using [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/) as a note-taking app.

Currently, this wiki's main focus is on [[Eurotour/Eurotour\|Eurotour]], a campaign ran on [startplaying.games](https://startplaying.games/adventure/cmihu4pwv00gkjr04blp7alqs) for [[Eurotour/Characters/Crew/The Crew\|The Crew]][^1] using [[Rules/CPRED/Cyberpunk RED\|Cyberpunk RED]] as the mechanical base and [[Cyberpunk 2020\|Cyberpunk 2020]] as its setting (with the added [Eurosource](https://www.drivethrurpg.com/en/product/25405/eurosource-plus) and [Eurotour](https://www.drivethrurpg.com/en/product/127101/eurotour-danger-death-on-a-euro-rock-tour) books).
