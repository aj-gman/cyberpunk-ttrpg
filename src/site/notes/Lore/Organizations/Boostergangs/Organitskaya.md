#boostergang 

This gangs are only the silver of the full scope. There are also Chechen, Azerbaijan, Armenian gangs etc. All of them have their traditions. Many of the Russian mafia gangs have connections in Europe. 

The government was very weak and many businesses were open. So this were the perfect terms for the rising of new criminal gangs. They didnt have the strict set of rules as the Thieves had. And were unscrupulous in their tactics. Many of them were pretty jacked and doing some sports. The streets in all the big cites were filled with violence end endless gang wars. 
#### Vanguard Brigade
- 2d6 Lenin posergang, born in leningrad
- make money by sports betting, fixing games etc. in order to "destroy capitalism"

#### French Gang
- 2d6 white-collar workers, born in moscow
- deal in highly illegal stims etc., not fully inducted in the "Thieves in Law", combat drugs and psychedelics, designer drugs

#### Retributioners
- 4d6 scavs, ex-doctors etc., mostly from armenia and georgia
- backstreet executioners, ripperdocs, organ/cyberware harvesters & doctors

#### EXKA
- d6 ex-KGB soldiers
- specialize in targeted assassinations and surveillance

#### Kerovski Brothers Syndicate
- 5d6 blue-collar workers with industrial [[Rules/CPRED/Cyberware/Cyberware\|Cyberware]]
- led by the remaining Kerovski brother, Chechen businessman
- specialize in construction and more "legal" business

## Thieves in Law
**"Vory"**
- d6 members with d6 guards protecting them
- highly respectable, older (40+), richer, more influential
- specialize in coordinating with [[Lore/Organizations/Corporations/SovOil\|SovOil]] and the [[Lore/Organizations/Europe/Neo-Sov\|Neo-Sov]] as well as *stealing very valuable assets*
