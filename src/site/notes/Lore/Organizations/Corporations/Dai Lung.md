#corporation 
*rip-offs of the most notorious guns, less reliable but sporting similar qualities;*

**Dai Lung Armories** is a weapons manufacturer in the _[Cyberpunk](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Cyberpunk_Series "Cyberpunk Series")_ universe. Usually, it has cheaper alternatives and riffs from [[Lore/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|Arasaka]] and [[Lore/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]], for which it has faced (and successfully evaded) numerous lawsuits. A longer-lasting alternative to [[Lore/Organizations/Corporations/Budget Arms\|Budget Arms]].
