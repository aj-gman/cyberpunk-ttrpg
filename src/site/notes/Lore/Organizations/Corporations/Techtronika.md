#corporation 
*devastating damage, but low clips, slow fire-rate, long reload; standard* 

Before the [Fourth Corporate War](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Corporate_War "Fourth Corporate War"), Techtronika was the Russian subsidiary of [Techtronica International](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Techtronica "Techtronica"). When Techtronica closed down after the war, Techtronika survived and became its own company. Known for their low cost, low maintenance, high-powered weaponry, Techtronika by [2077](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/2077 "2077") had expanded their product line with military robots and touted as the "Russian [[Lore/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]]".

Techtronika's arsenal is not the greatest available on the market, but it is simple, reliable and efficient. When it comes to putting an enemy down with brute force that never fails, Techtronika makes the weaponry of choice.
