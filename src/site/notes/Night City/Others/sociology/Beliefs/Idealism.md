Idealism in the [[Cyberpunk 2020\|Cyberpunk Universe]] refers to the belief that a #corporation is a force for positive change. 

**The core tenets of [[Night City/Others/sociology/Beliefs/Idealism\|Idealism]] are:**
- Corporations serve the interests of their workers better than other institutions (States, Labor Unions, NGOs etc.)
- Meritocracy is a real phenomenon - a diligent worker will always be rewarded for his effort.
- The Invisible Hand of the Market guides Corporate profit: those who try to play dirty (from [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Exec\|Execs]] to Corps) are punished sooner or later.

Those most likely to subscribe to the ideas put forth by [[Night City/Others/sociology/Beliefs/Idealism\|Idealism]] are [[Night City/Others/sociology/Strata/Corpzoner\|Corpzoners]], [[Night City/Others/sociology/Strata/Mover\|Movers]] and [[Night City/Others/sociology/Strata/Beaver\|Beavers]] - the three strata of [[Night City/Locations/Night City\|Night City]] that have been lucky enough to be recognized by the corporate machine and actually receive meaningful rewards from it for their loyalty. 

For an [[Night City/Others/sociology/Beliefs/Idealism\|Idealist]], an unfair valuation, a nasty boss or a bad order are mishaps that occur due to the human interference in the corporate machine. To that end, even if Corporations can self-regulate, bad actors can take advantage of the well-oiled cogs. But, say [[Night City/Others/sociology/Beliefs/Idealism\|Idealists]], all disruptions come to an end by way of the strong arm of the HR department.

[[Night City/Others/sociology/Beliefs/Idealism\|Idealists]] can be found across all Corporations, but often have to be tied to some sort of connection to said company - **loyalty via dependance, conditioning, education.** 
For example, many Corporations prefer to have **private schooling options** for their most loyal [[Night City/Others/sociology/Strata/Corpzoner\|Corpzoners]] in order to preemptively indoctrinate their children - [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|Arasaka School of Economics]], [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech Academy of Engineering]] and so on.
Loyalty can also be bought via supplying the worker with **a comfortable lifestyle** inevitably tied to the corporation. A [[Biotehnica\|Biotehnica]] [[Night City/Others/sociology/Strata/Beaver\|Beaver]] living in [[Rancho Coronado\|Rancho Coronado]] would stand to lose more than just their source of income if they quit or betrayed the Corp - they'd lose their house, their car, the respect of their peers.

### Further Reading
[[Night City/Others/sociology/Beliefs/Idealism\|Corporate Idealism]][^1] can be traced back to ideas like [Corporate Social Responsibility](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corporate_social_responsibility), [Socially Responsible Investing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socially_responsible_investing) and, most importantly, [Environmental, social, and governance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environmental,_social,_and_governance). These three principle posit that Corporations are responsible to self-regulate (and to be influenced by the stakeholders, i.e. their investors) in a positive manner.

[^1]: This ideology is most likely derived from the international relations philosophy of [Idealism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idealism_in_international_relations) most closely associated with US President [Woodrow Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woodrow_Wilson).
