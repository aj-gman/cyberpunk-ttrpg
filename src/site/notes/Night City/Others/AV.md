Commonly abbreviated as AV (pronounced "Ay-Vee"), an Aerodyne Vehicle is a vertical takeoff and landing (VTOL) aircraft powered by ducted jet fans.

It rose to prominence in the 2000s, when [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|Arasaka]] proved its effectiveness in an urban environment during the [[Night City/Locations/Night City#History\|Night City Mob Wars]]. Later, [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Trauma Team\|Trauma Team]] would begin using [[Night City/Others/AV\|AVs]] to extract high-value target from dangerous environments, which eventually led to mass production by companies like [[Zetatech\|Zetatech]], [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|Arasaka]], [[Rayfield\|Rayfield]] or [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]].
