#location 
The light industrial area of [[Night City/Locations/Night City\|Night City]], since the heavy industrial zones of [[Night City/Locations/Districts (2020)/South Night City\|South Night City]] were abandoned with the advent of the Mob Wars. Much of manufacturing is found here, from [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|Arasaka]] weapon factories to [[Zetatech\|Zetatech]] AV service shops.

[[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/El Norte Cartel\|El Norte Cartel]] has a strong presence in the neighborhood thanks to the numerous packaging warehouses they own in order to supply their product.
