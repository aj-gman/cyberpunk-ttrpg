Insurrectionism in the [[Rules/Cyberpunk Universe\|Cyberpunk Universe]] refers to the belief that all #corporation s are doomed to succumb to human greed and lust for power.

In terms of aesthetics, it is closely related to [[Night City/Beliefs/Fashion/Kitsch\|Kitsch]].

### Patrons
Although many [[Night City/Beliefs/Insurrectionism\|Insurrectionists]] look up to these individuals, they are not necessarily believers themselves.
