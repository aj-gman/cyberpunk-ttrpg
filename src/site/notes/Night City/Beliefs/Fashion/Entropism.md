Closely associated with [[Night City/Beliefs/Parallelism\|Parallelism]]. 
[[Night City/Beliefs/Parallelism\|Parallelists]] find alternate ways to sustain themselves, usually involving actions such as squatting, avoiding rent, sleeping in cars etc. As such, a culture of necessity has evolved together with the ideology.
