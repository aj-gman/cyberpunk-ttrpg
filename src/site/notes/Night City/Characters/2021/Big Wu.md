#npc #4cw [[Night City/Locations/Night City\|Night City]]
Artisan (Innovative, Trust) | Deliberate, Thoughtful, Flexible

Originally from Tokyo, Oshima Wu was born to a Hong Kong expat and a Japanese [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Exec\|Exec]]. He attended prestigious academies growing up and eventually got a [[Biotehnica\|Biotehnica]] certification allowing him to grow his own produce (under license).

[[Night City/Characters/2021/Big Wu\|Big Wu]] used to cook for the [[Crew/Hex\|Michaels family]]. Eventually, after the twins ([[Crew/Relevant NPCs/Natalie Michaels\|Natalie Michaels]] & [[Crew/Hex\|Hex]]) went to school, he took the savings he had, took a loan out from an AgriCorp ([[Continental Brands\|Continental Brands]]) and became a well-respected sushi chef with his own restaurant in the BosWash area. 

Eventually, however, [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Arasaka\|an Arasaka subsidiary]] bought out the joint and he was forced to become a franchisee. Things took a turn for the worst when the [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Exec\|Corpo]] who was managing his franchise eventually bought out all *profitable* franchisees and disenfranchised the rest. 

[[Night City/Characters/2021/Big Wu\|Big Wu]] was left with some cash, which he used to leave the area after being threatened by some East Coast MC with ties to the [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Tyger Clawz\|Tyger Clawz]].

Once in [[Night City/Locations/Night City\|Night City]], he used the connections he had with [[Night City/Organizations/Corporations/Militech\|Militech]] to buy out a small property in [[Night City/Locations/Districts (2020)/Little Asia\|Japantown]] and turn it into [[The Golden Palace\|The Golden Palace]].
By the [[Fourth Corporate War\|Fourth Corporate War]], [[Night City/Characters/2021/Big Wu\|Big Wu]] is a sought-out [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Fixer\|Fixer]] in his slice of the pie (i.e. [[Night City/Locations/Districts (2020)/Little Asia\|Japantown]]), with [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Tyger Clawz\|Tyger Clawz]] and [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Piranhas\|Piranhas]] fighting for gigs from him. He has some bad blood with the [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/Maelstrom\|Maelstrom]], since their presence in [[Night City/Locations/Districts (2020)/Little Asia\|Little Asia]] is taking a hit on "good business".

He first contacts [[Crew/The Crew\|The Crew]] upon their arrival in [[Night City/Locations/Night City\|Night City]] to offer them shelter and food. [[Crew/Hex\|Hex]]'s sister, [[Crew/Relevant NPCs/Natalie Michaels\|Natalie Michaels]], is friends with [[Night City/Characters/2021/Big Wu\|Big Wu]]. So he is playing at both ends.
