#boostergang [[Night City/Locations/Night City\|Night City]]
The Enhanced were a secretive cult in [Night City](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Night_City "Night City") whose members believed [cyberpsychosis](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Cyberpsychosis "Cyberpsychosis") was a form of enlightenment meant to represent the next stage of human evolution. As such, they were looking to accelerate this outcome by forcing society to be torn about by violence. Investigations concluded there was a direct connection with weapon manufacturer [GunMart](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/GunMart "GunMart") and its subsidiary [No/Brainer](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/No/Brainer "No/Brainer"), with cultist members either being heavily invested or simply owning the corporation.

Accelerationist, Tech, Netrunner
