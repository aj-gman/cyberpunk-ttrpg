#boostergang [[Night City/Locations/Night City\|Night City]] [[Combat Zone\|Combat Zone]]
By [2020](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/2020 "2020"), the Blood Razors in Night City were on top of the gang chain in the local combat zone, being considered one of the largest groups participating there. It was rumored that something called "RazorBall" was played in an abandoned ice-skating rink in the Zone once every month, which had a great turnout among the city gangs.
