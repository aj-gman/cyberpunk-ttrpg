>[!warning]- In 2077
A gang that controls the districts of [[Night City/Locations/Districts (2077)/Watson\|Watson]] and the [[Night City/Locations/Districts (2077)/Northside\|Northside]] Industrial District (NID). You can easily recognize them by their intimidating faceplates and hostile optic implants, which not only indicates their gang affiliation but also their fetishization of [[Rules/CPRED/Cyberware/Cyberware\|cyberware]] and cybermodification. Looking at Maelstrom, you might think, *"Now those are some hella bold pioneers of transhumanism."* If they were only testing the limits of the human body on themselves, then you'd be right... Sadly, they also like to conduct "experiments" on those unfortunate enough to cross their path.
Maelstrom is known for their exceptionally brutal and public gang executions – combine that with illegal braindance production and a manic fighting style and you've got yourself a gang with a reputation as a band of lunatics on the verge of [[Night City/Others/About Cyberpsychosis\|About Cyberpsychosis]]. The truth is there's nothing crazy about how Maelstrom operates – at the end of the day it's all about biz. And the more rumors there are about them, the better.
During the [[Night City/Gigs/2077/Electric Tornado\|"Electric Tornado" Crisis]], [[Night City/Characters/2077/Cross\|Cross]], [[Night City/Characters/2077/Warlock\|Warlock]] and [[Night City/Characters/2077/Bug\|Bug]], three Maelstrom higher-ups, were spotted negotiating with an up-and-coming Media from Pacifica, [[Night City/Characters/2077/Ryder\|Ryder]]. 

[^1]: There is a singular leader that controls the philosophy and the goals of the gang, with different subordinates being put in place to assure the good functioning of the chosen activities. Any of the leaders can be (and have been) dethroned by "might makes right" ideology, through duels, murders or street fights.

[^2]: Unverified claim supported only by independent [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Media\|Media]] investigations.
