[[Player Characters/The Gang\|The Gang]] is the faction started by [[Player Characters/OTHER/HEX_PUBLIC\|Hex]] and [[Player Characters/OTHER/RAY_PUBLIC\|Ray]] upon their arrival to [[Night City/Locations/Night City\|Night City]]. They were soon joined by [[Player Characters/Ze\|Ze]], a [[Rules/CPRED/Roles/Media\|Street reporter]] with ties to both [[Night City/Characters/2021/Big Wu\|Big Wu]] and [[Night City/Organizations/Gangs/The Mob\|The Mob]].
